# Website Improvement Project Tasks

## P0 — Homepage clarity and trust

### Rewrite homepage hero for clarity and conversion
**Purpose:** Make the homepage immediately explain who the foundation helps, what it does, and what action visitors should take.

### Add trust signals above the fold
**Purpose:** Increase credibility by showing registration details, founding year, contact information, and authentic visuals early on the homepage.

### Simplify website navigation
**Purpose:** Help visitors quickly find the most important sections by reducing the menu to a small set of clear navigation items.

### Build a complete footer with contact and legal information
**Purpose:** Improve trust, usability, and compliance by including contact details, social links, and legal pages in the footer.

### Improve mobile usability across the site
**Purpose:** Ensure the site is easy to read and navigate on phones, where many visitors are likely to access it.

## P1 — Core pages and organization credibility

### Create or improve About Us page
**Purpose:** Tell the foundation’s story clearly and communicate mission, vision, and values in a human, trustworthy way.

### Create or improve Team section
**Purpose:** Build credibility by showing the people behind the organization, including names, roles, and photos.

### Create or improve Programs page
**Purpose:** Clearly explain each initiative, who it serves, how it works, and how supporters can get involved.

### Create or improve Impact page
**Purpose:** Show measurable outcomes so visitors understand the real-world effect of the foundation’s work.

### Create or improve Contact page
**Purpose:** Make it easy for donors, volunteers, and partners to reach the organization through their preferred channel.

### Create transparency and legal information page
**Purpose:** Improve donor confidence and organizational legitimacy by sharing registration and donation-related information.

## P1 — Content quality and storytelling

### Rewrite website copy to be clear and concrete
**Purpose:** Replace vague language with simple messaging that explains actions, outcomes, and visitor value.

### Add testimonials and real community stories
**Purpose:** Build emotional trust and social proof through authentic examples from beneficiaries, volunteers, or partners.

### Add recent events and activity updates
**Purpose:** Show that the foundation is active and engaged through visible recent work, photos, and summaries.

### Add case studies or real examples of impact
**Purpose:** Demonstrate practical results through specific examples of community work and outcomes.

## P2 — Conversion and engagement

### Create or improve Donate page
**Purpose:** Make donating easier and more persuasive by explaining why to give, how funds are used, and how to complete the donation.

### Create or improve Volunteer page
**Purpose:** Convert interested visitors into volunteers by clearly explaining available roles and the signup process.

### Create or improve Partner With Us page
**Purpose:** Encourage schools, NGOs, sponsors, and collaborators to connect with a clear partnership pathway.

### Add CTAs throughout the site
**Purpose:** Improve conversions by placing donate, volunteer, and contact prompts in key content areas.

### Reduce form friction across contact, donation, and volunteer flows
**Purpose:** Increase completion rates by asking only for essential information and simplifying the experience.

## P2 — Visual design and UX polish

### Standardize typography, colors, and visual hierarchy
**Purpose:** Improve brand consistency and readability through a cleaner, more unified design system.

### Replace generic images with real photos
**Purpose:** Increase authenticity and connection by showing actual programs, people, and events.

### Improve spacing, readability, and section structure
**Purpose:** Make content easier to scan and understand by improving layout and reducing visual clutter.

### Improve button design and consistency
**Purpose:** Help users notice and understand actions by making buttons visually clear and consistent site-wide.

### Improve accessibility basics
**Purpose:** Make the site easier to use for more people by improving contrast, font sizing, and readability.

## P3 — SEO and discoverability

### Add page titles and meta descriptions across the site
**Purpose:** Improve search visibility and click-through rates by giving each page a clear, unique search snippet.

### Add alt text and image optimization
**Purpose:** Improve accessibility and SEO while also helping pages load faster.

### Add organization and contact schema
**Purpose:** Help search engines better understand the organization and display richer results where possible.

### Create blog, news, or updates section
**Purpose:** Keep the website fresh, support SEO, and showcase ongoing work over time.

### Improve social linking and discoverability
**Purpose:** Strengthen brand consistency and traffic flow by linking the website from all social profiles and platforms.
