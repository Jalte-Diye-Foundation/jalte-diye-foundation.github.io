# Team Management

## Team Composition

- 3 teams
- 3 members per team

## Teams

- Team 1: Development
- Team 2: Deployment
- Team 3: Documentation

## Team Artifacts

Each team should maintain:

- Active issue list
- Weekly progress summary
- Open blocker list
- Sprint completion notes

## Roles Inside Each Team

- Coordinator: planning and communication
- Implementer: primary executor for current tasks
- Reviewer: internal quality check before PR

Roles should rotate each sprint.

## Cross-Team Handoffs

- Team 1 to Team 2 for release-impacting changes.
- Team 1 to Team 3 for user-facing text changes.
- Team 3 to Team 1 when docs uncover implementation gaps.
