Our Projects and Initiatives

Innovation in Action

These projects and initiatives represent our current focus areas for long-term social impact.

We are combining healthcare, education, environment, technology, and mental well-being interventions to build practical and sustainable change.

1. Deepak Jyoti Clinic

Deepak Jyoti Clinic is a healthcare-focused initiative based in Jaipur, serving local community members through inexpensive/free consultation and routine care support.

We provide public healthcare services which reflects our values of accessible and service-oriented community support.

Location focus: Jaipur, Rajasthan

2. Really Real Education

ReallyRealEducation.org is our education content initiative focused on meaningful learning, civic awareness, and personal growth through social education.

The platform aligns with our foundation values by encouraging reflective thinking, responsible citizenship, and dialogue-based understanding for long-term social harmony.

Initiative type: Free/affordable Education content and awareness platform #SDG4

3. Miyawaki Forestation Project

This project promotes dense native plantations using the Miyawaki method to accelerate green cover restoration in limited urban and semi-urban spaces. #SDG13

Our goal is to improve local biodiversity, strengthen climate resilience, and create community-owned ecological assets.

4. Cognitive Agentic AI System for Social Education

This initiative is focused on building an AI-assisted educational ecosystem for social learning, civic responsibility, and reflective thinking. #SDG4

The system is designed to enable personalized guidance, interactive learning flows, and accessible knowledge support for diverse communities.

5. Smart Wearable for Diagnosis and Improvement of Mental Health

This project explores a smart wearable solution to support early indicators, monitoring, and lifestyle-based interventions for mental well-being. #SDG4

The vision is to combine responsible sensing, data-informed insights, and awareness-led support to improve mental health outcomes.

How These Efforts Support Our Mission

  • Promote practical social welfare through local healthcare support
  • Build awareness and responsible thinking through educational content
  • Advance environment, technology, and mental well-being solutions for sustainable impact
