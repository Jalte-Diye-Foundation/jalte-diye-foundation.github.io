# Governance

This document defines how project decisions are made and how contributors can
grow into broader responsibilities.

## Roles

- Maintainers: Review and merge pull requests, manage releases, and enforce
  project standards.
- Contributors: Submit issues, pull requests, documentation updates, design
  updates, and content improvements.
- Community members: Use the project, share feedback, and help improve quality.

## Team Model

The repository currently operates with three working teams:

- Team 1 (Development): Feature work and bug fixes through `team1-dev` and `dev`.
- Team 2 (Deployment): Release and deployment work through `team2-deploy` and `deploy`.
- Team 3 (Documentation): Docs and wiki work through `team3-doc` and `doc`.

Each team has 3 members and one rotating coordinator responsible for weekly
status updates.

## Decision Making

- Day-to-day changes are decided by maintainers through pull request review.
- For major changes, maintainers seek feedback in issues/discussions first.
- If consensus is not clear, maintainers make the final decision.

## Contribution Priorities

Priority is generally given to:

- Security and trust-related fixes.
- Accessibility and usability improvements.
- Accuracy of public information and policy pages.
- Reliability and maintainability.

## Becoming A Maintainer

Contributors may be invited to become maintainers based on:

- Quality and consistency of contributions.
- Constructive collaboration and communication.
- Understanding of project goals and standards.

## Communication Principles

- Keep project discussions public where possible.
- Be respectful and transparent.
- Use `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md` as the baseline for interactions.
