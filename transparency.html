6. 12A / 80G Certificates

Tax exemption certificates under Sections 12A and 80G of the Income Tax Act, 1961 allow donors to claim deductions on contributions made to registered NGOs.

  • 12A Registration: Application in process. Donors will be notified once registration is confirmed.
  • 80G Certification: Donations are currently not eligible for 80G tax deduction. We are working towards obtaining this certification.

Once obtained, certificates will be available for download on this page. Donors requiring tax acknowledgment letters in the interim may write to contact@jaltediyefoundation.org.
