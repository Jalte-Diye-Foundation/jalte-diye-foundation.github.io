About JDF

What is JDF?

JDF stands for Jalte Diye Foundation.

Jalte Diye Foundation (JDF) is a nonprofit organization working to advance global peace through holistic social education. We promote awareness against discrimination, encourage environmental responsibility, and support education and community development through initiatives that inspire informed thinking, responsible citizenship, and positive social change.

Our Mission and Vision

Mission: Providing social education globally to the humans for peaceful life and harmonious relations.

Vision: We envision a world in which the entire human population cooperates as a family for global peace.

Read more about our philosophy and objectives on the homepage.

Organizational Identity

  • Legal name: Jalte Diye Foundation
  • Also known as: JDF
  • Type: Section 8 Company under the Companies Act, 2013 (India)
  • Based in: Jaipur, Rajasthan, India
  • Official website: jaltediyefoundation.org

Registration and governance details are published on our Transparency and Reports page.

What We Work On

  • Social education: learning that builds informed thinking and responsible citizenship.
  • Anti-discrimination awareness: interactive sessions that help reduce inequalities and encourage participation.
  • Environmental action: climate awareness and Miyawaki-based plantation efforts.
  • Community development: outreach and support for local communities.
  • Global peace: discussion-based learning about rights, responsibilities, peace and justice.

These areas are aligned with UN Sustainable Development Goals 4, 10, 13 and 16.

Our Projects

Our initiatives include Deepak Jyoti Clinic, Really Real Education, the Miyawaki Forestation Project and technology-led social education efforts. See details on our Projects page, and browse activity photos in the Gallery.
