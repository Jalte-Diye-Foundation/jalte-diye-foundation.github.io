How It Works

Complete workflow for issuing and managing certificates.

  • 1

    Collect volunteer details

    Gather the volunteer's full name, the event they participated in, their role, and the date of issue.

  • 2

    Generate certificate entry

    Go to Add Certificate, fill in the details, and click "Generate Certificate Entry". The system auto-assigns a unique ID in the format JDF-{EVENT}-CE{SEQ}.

  • 3

    Download & commit updated data.json

    Click "Download Updated data.json". Commit this file to your GitHub repository at certificates/data.json. This makes the certificate verifiable immediately (without PDF).

  • 4

    Upload the PDF certificate

    Place the volunteer's PDF certificate in the repo at certificates/pdfs/{ID}.pdf — e.g. certificates/pdfs/JDF-FDC-CE01.pdf. Then update hasPdf to true in data.json and commit.

  • 5

    Share the certificate link

    Send the volunteer their public URL: https://jalte-diye-foundation.github.io/certificate.html?id={ID} or the verify page at /verify.html.

  • 6

    Revoking a certificate

    In the Certificates tab, click "Remove" on the entry. Download the updated data.json and commit it. The certificate ID will return "not found" on the verify page.
