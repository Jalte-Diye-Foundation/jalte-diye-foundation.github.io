Verify a Certificate

Enter the Certificate ID printed on the certificate to confirm its authenticity.

Certificate IDs follow the format JDF-{EVENT}-CE{NUMBER} and are printed at the bottom of your certificate.

📋

What is a Certificate ID?

A unique identifier in the format JDF-{EVENT}-CE{NUMBER} printed on every certificate we issue.

✅

What does verification confirm?

Verification confirms the certificate was officially issued by Jalte Diye Foundation and its details are authentic.

📧

Certificate not found?

If your ID is not recognised, please contact us at contact@jaltediyefoundation.org
