Mission in Action

Our foundation mission is translated into practical action through focused initiatives.

These initiatives represent our combined effort in healthcare access, social awareness, and education-driven community development.

Deepak Jyoti Clinic, Kartarpura, Jaipur

Deepak Jyoti Clinic is a healthcare-focused initiative based in Kartarpura, Jaipur, serving local community members through inexpensive/free consultation and routine care support.

We provide public healthcare services which reflects our values of accessible and service-oriented community support.

Location focus: Hanuman Colony, Kartarpura, Jaipur, Rajasthan

View location on Google Maps

Really Real Education

ReallyRealEducation.org is our education content initiative focused on meaningful learning, civic awareness, and personal growth through social education.

The platform aligns with our foundation values by encouraging reflective thinking, responsible citizenship, and dialogue-based understanding for long-term social harmony.

Initiative type: Education content and awareness platform #SDG4
